Served as the teaching assistant for multiple sections of SC 332 - Color and Light at the Fashion Institute.
In this course the basic principles of color science, including color measurement and color reproduction, are examined.
I mainly focused on aiding students in using scientific equipment such as colorimeters and Vis-NIR spectrometers, among others, to translate theory into practice.
