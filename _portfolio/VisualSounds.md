**VisualSounds** is the first building stone of a larger idea I'm assembling quietly.

What I'm sharing now is the foundation: an iOS system that detects **where your eyes are looking on the screen**, built with the iPhone TrueDepth / Face ID camera stack, ARKit face tracking, personalized on-device gaze calibration, and live inference. Think of it as learning to read attention itself — fixations, saccades, looking away — from the device you already hold.

What it unlocks is still under wraps. I'm only giving glimpses: a new kind of **media consumption** experience, one that listens to where attention lands and answers back through **text-to-audio transformers**. More soon.

For now, the stone on the table is gaze — precise, personal, on-device.
